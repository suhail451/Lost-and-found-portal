Tips for a good report

  • 🎨Mention colour, brand and any unique marks, stickers or scratches.
  • 📍Say where and roughly when it happened.
  • 🔒Don't post ID, card or account numbers. Keep a detail only the owner would know.
  • ✅Mark the item as returned once it's sorted, so others stop searching.
